ASSIGNED 3 VALIDATION & BUSINESS ERRORS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Error Code: ERR_PROD_INVALID_NAME
   Name:      Blank Product Name Validation Error
   Condition: Attempting to publish an instrument listing with an empty or whitespace name.
   Message:   "ERR_PROD_INVALID_NAME: Product name cannot be blank."

2. Error Code: ERR_PROD_INVALID_PRICE
   Name:      Non-Positive Retail Price Error
   Condition: Pricing set to less than or equal to LKR 0.00.
   Message:   "ERR_PROD_INVALID_PRICE: Product retail price must be greater than zero."

3. Error Code: ERR_PROD_INVALID_DISCOUNT
   Name:      Promotional Discount Range Policy Violation
   Condition: Discount percentage configured below 0% or exceeding 100%.
   Message:   "ERR_PROD_INVALID_DISCOUNT: Promotional discount percentage must be between 0% and 100%."
