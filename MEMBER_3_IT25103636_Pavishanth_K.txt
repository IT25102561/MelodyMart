ASSIGNED 3 VALIDATION & BUSINESS ERRORS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Error Code: ERR_CART_INVALID_QTY
   Name:      Non-Positive Cart Quantity Rejection
   Condition: Entering quantity of zero or negative integers for cart items.
   Message:   "ERR_CART_INVALID_QTY: Quantity must be at least 1."

2. Error Code: ERR_CART_INSUFFICIENT_STOCK
   Name:      Warehouse Stock Exceeded Breach
   Condition: Attempting to add more units of an instrument than available in warehouse stock.
   Message:   "ERR_CART_INSUFFICIENT_STOCK: Only [X] units available in warehouse."

3. Error Code: ERR_CART_INVALID_VOUCHER
   Name:      Unrecognized or Expired Voucher Code
   Condition: Customer submits an expired or non-existent promotional coupon.
   Message:   "ERR_CART_INVALID_VOUCHER: Promo voucher '[code]' is expired or invalid."
