ASSIGNED 3 VALIDATION & BUSINESS ERRORS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Error Code: ERR_USER_DUPLICATE_EMAIL
   Name:      Duplicate Email Registration Conflict
   Condition: Triggered when registering with an email already present in the user database.
   Message:   "ERR_USER_DUPLICATE_EMAIL: An account with email '[email]' is already registered."

2. Error Code: ERR_USER_PASSWORD_STRENGTH
   Name:      Password Complexity Policy Violation
   Condition: Password submitted has fewer than 8 characters or lacks both letters and digits.
   Message:   "ERR_USER_PASSWORD_STRENGTH: Password must be at least 8 characters long and contain both letters and numbers."

3. Error Code: ERR_USER_INVALID_PHONE
   Name:      Invalid Sri Lankan Mobile Format
   Condition: Phone number does not conform to Sri Lankan telecommunication standards (07XXXXXXXX or +947XXXXXXXX).
   Message:   "ERR_USER_INVALID_PHONE: Invalid Sri Lankan mobile format. Expected 07XXXXXXXX or +947XXXXXXXX."
