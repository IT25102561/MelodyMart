   Message:   "ERR_SUP_INVALID_NAME: Supplier company name is required."

2. Error Code: ERR_SUP_INVALID_EMAIL

   Condition: Supplier contact email fails basic RFC email format syntax validation.
   Message:   "ERR_SUP_INVALID_EMAIL: Valid contact email is required."

   Name:      Non-Positive Purchase Order Quantity/Valuation
   Condition: Requisition submitted with zero quantity or non-positive total cost.
   Message:   "ERR_PO_INVALID_AMOUNTS: Quantity and total amount must be positive."
