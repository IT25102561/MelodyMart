ASSIGNED 3 VALIDATION & BUSINESS ERRORS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Error Code: ERR_PAY_INVALID_AMOUNT
   Name:      Non-Positive Transaction Value Error
   Condition: Attempting to initiate a payment transaction of zero or negative LKR.
   Message:   "ERR_PAY_INVALID_AMOUNT: Payment amount must be positive."

2. Error Code: ERR_PAY_METHOD_REQUIRED
   Name:      Unspecified Payment Channel Omission
   Condition: Submitting checkout without choosing an active Sri Lankan payment method.
   Message:   "ERR_PAY_METHOD_REQUIRED: Please select a valid Sri Lankan payment method."

3. Error Code: ERR_PAY_UNVERIFIED_SLIP
   Name:      Missing Bank Slip Attachment
   Condition: Bank Transfer payment selected without providing deposit slip reference.
   Message:   "ERR_PAY_UNVERIFIED_SLIP: Please attach a bank deposit receipt slip reference."
